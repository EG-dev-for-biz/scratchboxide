## 2. The method (what pipeline K is)

`packages/core/src/node/3d-engine/staged-location/pipeline-k/`. On the remeshed scan (tile_refine: facades snapped to fitted planes; remesh polish cleanup), per rectified facade ortho (`relief_orthos/`):

1. **Lines**: column and row edge-energy peaks (as `facade-lattice.ts`), each with its edge polarity.
2. **Curves**: each line traced along its length by Viterbi over offsets within 0.35 m (reward: the signed gradient of its own polarity; cost per pixel of bend), sub-pixel by a parabola through the reward; split where the edge is lost for more than 4 m, each segment straightened to its own weighted median (a real offset between tiers is never forced into one line).
3. **Warp**: a content-preserving mesh warp, the line-collinearity and triangle-similarity terms of Li et al. 2017 ("Image stitching by line-guided local warping with global similarity constraint", eqs. 10 and 12; the same three terms TwinTex uses for facade photos), plus a weak anchor and a pinned border. Jacobi-PCG on the normal equations; scaled back toward the identity if any triangle would fold.
4. **Smears**: the facade's bay pitch from the column profile's autocorrelation (as Liu, Psarakis and Stamos 2013 estimate facade periods); each pixel's repeats at +-1 and +-2 pitches, each phase-searched +-2 px and kept only where the local NCC (gain-invariant) is at least 0.75 (the PatchMatch lattice paper's NCC gate); the low-passed, vertically elongated log ratio to their median is levelled where it is beyond 3 robust sd, gain only, bounded.
5. **Verified per facade**: kept only when the traced lines get at least 20% closer to straight and the lattice homogeneity (median per-pixel spread across repeats, Liu et al. 2015) does not get worse by more than 2%.
6. The rectified orthos (cached beside the originals, keyed by version and parameters) go to the remesh polish, which rebuilds each facade on the lines its photo shows (`facade-lattice.ts`) and textures it with that photo. For K the texture stage uses tile_refine's own twin of that GLB (a texture-pass re-bake would undo the straightening). Pipeline E and the facade grammar are skipped for K.

## 4. What Broke / Didn't Work

* **Run 1: textures would have been re-baked from the tile.** For an isolated structure the texture stage runs the texture pass, which re-projects the tile photo and undoes the straightened facades. Fixed: K uses tile_refine's twin (`orchestrator.ts`, texture `mode`).
* **Run 2: flat grey faces in recessed bays** (`esb_k_run2_grey_recess_defect.jpg`). `facade-lattice.ts` rebuilt every cell of a facade's lattice, including cells where the ortho is uncovered (exact grey 128: a recess deeper than the ortho slab), and painted them grey. This affects the default lattice path too; it had never run on the ESB. Fixed (`facade-lattice/2`): a cell is rebuilt only when at least half its ortho is covered; elsewhere the scan's triangles stay and no step face is drawn against it (1,389 ESB cells now keep their scan).
* **First smear version damaged non-repeating structure** (setback walls, terraces darkened in pier-wide blocks). Fixed by NCC-gated, phase-searched repeats; after the fix the gain map shows only isolated corrections.
* **Not fixed / open:**
  - The ESB orthos themselves were already within about 1 px (6-7 cm) of straight; most of the visible straightening comes from rebuilding the facades flat on the photo (the scan's wobbly triangles replaced), not from the warp.
  - 31 of 83 facades are below the lattice's 0.6 coverage and keep their remeshed scan.
  - The tile_refine twin de-lights and (with a model client) recreates the photo, so colours read lighter and greyer than the raw tile.
  - The orbit camera zooms toward the scene target, not the cursor, so close framings take several tries.
  - Two ortho planes (`facade_0`, `facade_1`) are podium roof planes stretched as facades by tile_refine; they are below the straightening gate and were left as they were.

## 4b. Follow-up the same day: the 3D Building button, holes and tears

* **3D Building runs K on the ESB.** The button now sends one prompt (the Empire State Building, straightened from Google Maps); `parseStagedLocationArgs` makes pipeline K the default for one building (`procedural: true` or a procedural `pipeline` still selects J/I/H/G). In the app the button's run (`cf78b518`) carried `{"isolate":true,"pipeline":"k"}`.
* **Holes in the original texturing** (grey wedges, the ortho's uncovered texels, on rebuilt walls): the rectifier now fills them last, from the facade's own repeats at its bay pitch, then its storey pitch, then the nearest real texel (never a fill from a fill), and writes the uncovered mask beside the ortho; the rebuild reads coverage from that mask (a filled cell the photo never reached is not built) and labels the fill `generated` in the facade's GLB extras. ESB: 1,653,225 uncovered texels filled, 79% from repeats.
* **Tears** (see-through slits around the base entrance and at corners): a scan triangle is now removed only when every rebuilt facade it faces covers it in its own rectangle; a tear audit renders each facade straight on (scan against result, 0.2 m) and puts the scan back over any torn pixel, up to three rounds, then leaves a still-torn facade as scanned. The first in-app version dropped 12 facades and left 39,375 torn pixels (a coverage rule that let a parallel tier vouch for another's triangles, and an audit that counted walls far in front as the facade's own); fixed and checked offline on the same ESB remesh: 353 torn pixels found, 989 triangles put back, 0 facades dropped, 0 left, 53 facades rebuilt, 9.4 s.
* **Texture patches** (the user's close-up of the tower: blocks of different tone and sharpness). Measured on run `9310bb3b`: all 189 photos were de-lit, then the twin sent each to `gemini-3.1-flash-image` to be recreated; 28 were accepted (2 facade orthos, 26 leaf tiles; identified by re-applying the de-light to each source photo) and 161 rejected, so neighbouring photos came from two different processes. Fixed for K: no recreate (de-light only), then `texture-harmonize.ts`: a gain per photo toward a reference per side (8 compass bins, up, down) and 15 m height band, and seam levelling (Lempitsky and Ivanov 2007): a smooth offset field per photo fitted where two photos share a 1.5 m patch of surface facing the same way. In the app (run `d0832981`, from the 3D Building button): 27,326 shared patches, mean patch difference 0.129 -> 0.100 in log colour (23% lower), 11.6 s; `esb_k_harmonised_close.jpg` shows the two tower faces in one tone. Measured offline: the per-band gain alone raises the seam difference (photo-wide gains pull neighbours apart), so it runs at half strength and the levelling follows; texel-level seam pairs were tried first and dropped (window-or-pier detail dominates them).
* **Stray pieces around the base** (`base-cleanup.ts`, the last step of K's remesh polish, run note `pipeline_k_base.json`): by the footprint the run resolved (Google vector footprint for the ESB, which fits: 0% of the building above 3 m outside it). Detached low pieces outside it go (none on the ESB: the isolate had already dropped them; after cleanup no piece stands more than 1.5 m outside the footprint); the building's triangles below 3 m outside it by more than 0.3 m go (low down by any corner, since 1-2 m remesh triangles left a sawtooth by their centres); the street itself (surfaces facing up within 0.6 m of the ground) goes everywhere; the base is closed by a generated skirt along each edge's measured wall line where a wall stands near. Checked offline on the ESB remesh with clay renders from fixed cameras, version by version: a wall-line inset (in case the footprint was wider than the walls) measured 0 on all 26 edges; the remaining jagged strip was the street at grade inside the footprint, which the grade rule took out. Result: 2,269 apron triangles (1,681 m²) and 1,602 street triangles (4,773 m²) removed, skirt 62 m² on 10 edges, 1.3 s. A few thin slivers remain near two corners.
* **Top-down view of the de-lit, harmonised twin (Status: Needs Fix).** The texture step was only ever checked from the side. Run `62293aaf` (twin `empire_state_building_new_york_iso_l2_tex_4274c1bc.glb`) rendered straight down in the app (`docs/screenshots/esb_k_topdown_full.jpg`, `esb_k_topdown_crown.jpg`): the sun is still baked into the roofs. The terrace ring around the tower is grey-blue on the side the tower shades and salmon on the sunlit side; the tower's cast shadow lies across the upper-left terraces; near-black wedges sit between the crown's buttresses; hard-edged rectangles of different tone (single photos) sit on the terrace ring; and the tops of the upper walls, seen from above, read washed-out white. Causes in code: the de-light is a luma curve that ignores facing and sun (a lit roof and a shaded roof get the same curve); harmonise matches up-facing surfaces only within one 15 m band, so separate terraces are never matched to each other; its seam levelling never compares a roof patch with the wall beside it (separate facing bins); and every photo covering a roof also covers walls (123 of 123), so a photo's one gain is set mostly by its walls.
* **Roofs fixed (Status: Verified in the app, run `21f9c11c`, twin `..._l2_tex_a005328f.glb`).** A top-down ortho of the raw tile photos (`esb_k_ortho_raw.png`) showed the roofs were right before our step: maroon podium roofs, salmon terraces, green gardens. Our step did the damage: the shadow lift pulled every dark texel toward grey (a dark roof is a dark material, not a shadow), and the per-photo gain laid rectangles over flat roofs. Also, 77% of the up-facing samples had geometry straight above them (hidden shells of the remesh), skewing the roof references. A sun fit to the roof shadows was tried first and dropped: it landed at 79° elevation, higher than the sun reaches over New York. Now (`texture-harmonize/2`): each texel is classed roof (up-facing, nothing over it on a top-down 0.5 m height map, tested per texel) or wall; roof texels get highlight compression but no shadow lift; roof and wall each get their own gain per photo; hidden up-facing surfaces (7,666 m²) are left out of the statistics; roof seen from above is 8,230 m². Top-down ortho, against the raw photos: saturation 0.455 raw, 0.160 before, 0.440 after; mean log difference from raw 0.908 before, 0.195 after. Seams 0.156 -> 0.112. In-app top-down renders: `esb_k_topdown_full_fixed.jpg`, `esb_k_topdown_crown_fixed.jpg`. Still as captured: the tower's cast shadow on the upper-left terraces (dark, not blue-grey any more) and the black wells between the crown's buttresses (black in the raw photo too). The upper setback walls still read pale from above: they are walls, whose de-light is unchanged.

## 5. Candidate Tool/Service Ideas

* **A camera preset tool for staged builds** (oblique / front / close on a tier, by height): this session framed the result by trial-and-error scrolls and drags.
* **A before/after slider for staged layers** in the viewport (remesh vs polished): the comparison had to be made from separate runs and offline ortho diffs.
* **Ortho coverage per facade in the build summary**: which facades were too uncovered to rebuild, so a user sees why some walls stay as scanned.
