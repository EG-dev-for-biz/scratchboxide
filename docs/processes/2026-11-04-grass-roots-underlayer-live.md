# Process: Dark roots and a blade-textured underlayer so the lawn reads dense, live

> **Recorded:** 2026-11-04
> **Status:** Verified live at Sheep Meadow; first underlayer shade (0.55) too dark, tuned to 0.78 in-session
> **Tags:** `operate-scratchbox` `grass` `native-engine` `shading`
> **Target Domain:** Staged-location evidence tools (`location_grass_map` blades) / 3D viewport

---

## 1. Session Context

* **What prompted this session:** the user asked what tricks make grass feel as dense as reality, then chose the first two: dark roots (self-occlusion) and a layer under the blades so no bare ground shows between them.
* **Instance used:** web `3170`, API `3171`, tools `3172` (own launch entry, removed afterwards). Sheep Meadow was freshly extracted; each build ran with load under 100.
* **Scope tested:** `location_grass_map` with `bladeRootShade: 1, bladeUnderlayerRadiusM: 0` (before) and with the defaults (after). The same 150,000 clumps and camera in every run (eye 1.7 m at the band centre, 3 m and 12 m).

---

## 2. What Was Tested & What Worked (Verified)

* **Dark roots** (`scratchbox_grass_field.hpp`): each blade's vertex tone climbs from `rootShade` 0.3 at the root to full at `occlusionHeight` 0.6 of the blade. This replaces the old pale sheath.
* **Underlayer:**
  * a sheet 2 cm over the grass cells within 40 m of the centre, standing on the scan's surface;
  * its colour is the cell's photo colour darkened to `underlayerShade` 0.78, and by `contactShade` 0.25 where clumps stand;
  * it fades back to the photo colour over 1.5 m at the grass edge and over the last quarter of its radius;
  * it carries a generated tiling blade-stroke texture (256 px per 0.5 m);
  * it is one textured node beside the instanced blades, labelled generated, colour photo-inferred.
* **Sheep Meadow:** 4,923 m² of underlayer, 157 k triangles; the total goes from 3.83 to 3.99 M. With the underlayer on, the tile photo's bright, blurry gaps between the tufts are gone and the ground reads as continuous turf.
* Proof sheet: `docs/screenshots/2026-11-04-grass-roots-underlayer-proof.png` (gitignored, local).

---

## 3. What Broke / Didn't Work

* **First underlayer shade 0.55 was too dark.** The photo colour of a lawn seen from above already averages blades and shaded gaps, so darkening it by half counted the shade twice, and the blades read as pale straw against a dark floor. It was tuned in-session to 0.78, with contact 0.25 and senescence 0.2, plus a fade at the rim; the 40 m edge had shown as a band.
* **Open:** the tile photo's large blotches still show through the underlayer's colour, as broad light and dark patches. That is the photo's own variation, kept as photo-inferred colour.

---

## 4. Candidate Tool/Service Ideas

* **Shells over the near lawn** (the next density trick): stacked transparent layers of blade cross-sections, generated by the engine, for a few metres around the viewer.

## 5. Reproduction Steps

1. Start an instance; delete `public/models/sheep_meadow_*`.
2. Run `location_grass_map` with `{"query":"Sheep Meadow, Central Park, New York, NY","radiusMeters":160,"blades":true,"bladeBandRadiusM":120}`, and again with `"bladeRootShade":1,"bladeUnderlayerRadiusM":0` for the old look.
3. Compare at eye height from the centre, setting the view repeatedly until the camera stops moving.
