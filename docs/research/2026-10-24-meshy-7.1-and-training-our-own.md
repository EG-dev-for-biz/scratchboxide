# Meshy 7.1: what's known, what's inferred, and could we train our own?

Researched 2026-10-24. Meshy publishes no papers or weights. Everything marked **inferred** is reasoning from public outputs and from the open models in the same field.

## 1. History (public record)

| Version | When | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| Meshy-1 to 3 | 2023–24 | Text/image-to-3D via score distillation and multi-view diffusion. Blobby results. |
| Meshy-4/5 | 2024–25 | Big jump in geometry. Likely a native 3D latent model, plus PBR textures and remeshing. |
| Meshy 6 | preview 2025-10-16, GA 2026-01-18 | "Sculpting-level" detail, Low Poly mode, multi-colour printing. No technical paper. |
| Meshy 7 | 2026 | Marketed as an "image-to-3D alignment engine": better faithfulness to the input image. |
| Meshy 7.1 | rollout 2026-09-10, GA 2026-09-16; API 2026-09-18 | A new detail generation stage on top of 7's image understanding. Ultra 2K = 2048³ (single and multi-view, 1–4 views); Ultra 4K = 4096³ (single view only); raw meshes up to about 80M triangles before simplification. Same API cost as 7. |

**Company:** founder Ethan Hu (MIT PhD); 12M+ users; ARR above $100M; Series B of nearly $400M at a $1.5B valuation (July 2026). Also working on "Mora", a research demo where a world is built (agents + Meshy 3D) and then rendered by a real-time video model.

**Meshy's own benchmark ("Detail Richness", internal):** 7.1 ranks first at 1024/2048/4096 render sizes against Tripo 3.1, Hunyuan3D 3.1, Rodin 2.5 and Hi3D 3.0, but only by about 1–2 points (for example 23.1% vs 21.9% at 4096). The win is real but narrow, and self-reported.

## 2. Reverse-engineered pipeline (inferred)

The only stated architecture detail is "a scaled-up geometry representation and generation backbone that handles billions of voxels" plus "high-precision mesh extraction and simplification". That matches the sparse-voxel structured latent family (TRELLIS / TRELLIS.2 O-Voxel, Hunyuan3D 3.x, Direct3D-S2):

1. **Image understanding:** a big vision encoder (DINO-class) produces image tokens; for multi-view, each view's tokens carry a view embedding. Meshy 7's "alignment" was probably this stage, plus training on rendered image→asset pairs with exact camera matching.
2. **Coarse structure:** a flow-matching DiT predicts which sparse voxels are occupied at low resolution (about 64³).
3. **Detail cascade (the 7.1 addition):** one or more super-resolution / detail stages that refine the sparse voxels up to 2048³ or 4096³. Only occupied surface cells are stored, which is why "billions of voxels" is affordable. 4K being single-view only suggests the detail stage was trained on (or is limited to) one view's conditioning: memory, or no multi-view data at that resolution.
4. **Mesh extraction:** dual contouring or flexible marching cubes on the fine field → about 80M triangles → QEM-style simplification and quad remeshing.
5. **Texture:** a separate multi-view PBR diffusion stage + back-projection + UV inpaint (not changed in 7.1).

**Why it's good (inferred, in order of likely weight):**
- **Data:** curated, licensed, cleaned high-detail assets. Probably bought or commissioned sculpt-grade meshes. This is the moat; Objaverse is mostly low-detail.
- **Resolution cascade:** coarse-to-fine sparse latents let them spend compute only on the surface.
- **Engineering on the output:** extraction, simplification, symmetry, retopology. Much of the "clean" look comes from here.
- **Scale and feedback:** about $100M ARR pays for compute and human preference data (12M users' downloads and regenerations act as an implicit reward signal).

## 3. Could we train TRELLIS to be as good?

**TRELLIS.2-4B** (Microsoft, MIT licence): O-Voxel sparse structure, 4B flow-matching transformer, 16× VAE downsampling (about 9.6K tokens at 1024³). Its model card says it is not preference-aligned or aesthetically fine-tuned. That's exactly the gap Meshy has filled.

| Goal | Feasible? | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| Fine-tune TRELLIS.2 for our domain (buildings fitted to tiles, hydrant-class props) | **Yes** | A few thousand high-quality domain assets, about 8×H100 for days to weeks. We already generate measured scan targets: that's a training-pair source nobody else has. |
| Add multi-view conditioning (our 2×2 character sheet) | **Yes, medium** | Extend the conditioning with per-view embeddings and train on rendered 4-view sets. Hunyuan3D already does this. |
| Preference/DPO alignment on outputs | **Yes** | Our photo-match judge and the Haughwout benchmark are ready-made reward signals. |
| Match Meshy 7.1's 4096³ detail generally | **Not realistically** | It needs a new detail-cascade stage, a curated dataset of millions of sculpt-grade assets, and a cluster-scale training run (likely thousands of GPU-months). This is a company-scale effort. |

Training code status: the TRELLIS (v1) repo shipped training code. For TRELLIS.2 I could not confirm released training or fine-tuning scripts from search results. Check `github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS.2` before planning.

## 4. Could a Stable Diffusion model do it?

Not as the 3D generator. SD is a 2D image model. The routes through it are:
- **SD/SDXL → multi-view images → reconstruction** (Zero123++, MVDream, Wonder3D style). This was Meshy's 2023 approach, and it is what it moved away from: inconsistency between views makes the geometry soft.
- **SD as the texture stage** (multi-view PBR diffusion). Good use, but we already have Nano Banana for this.
- Our Pipeline I (character sheet → Meshy) is already "2D model for views, 3D model for geometry". An SD route would just be a weaker version of the first half.

## 5. Recommendation

Keep Meshy 7.1 (Ultra 2K multi-view) as the Pipeline I default for general geometry. If we invest in training, the strongest bet is a **domain fine-tune of TRELLIS.2 on our own scan-fitted building data, with the photo-match judge as a DPO reward**. We could plausibly beat Meshy *on our buildings* (likeness to the real place) without beating it in general. Don't train an SD-based 3D path.

Note: AGENTS.md lists neural image-to-3D (Trellis/TripoSR) outside the Fal.ai path as unsupported. Running a self-trained TRELLIS in the app would need that rule changed first.
