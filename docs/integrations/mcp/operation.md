### `operation_cancel`

Request cancellation of Basic image/video (including Gemini Omni Prompt Studio), character 3D, audio, writing, or Presentation media operations. Gemini Omni jobs are non-resumable; an interrupted provider call is failed and refunded rather than replayed. Upscale cancellation is unavailable because its provider call cannot be safely aborted. Poll operation.get for the terminal status.
