# 13-Section Director's Treatment Master Template (Paul Regan Style)

Use this canonical template when writing or rewriting a commercial director's treatment. 
Every major section header **MUST BE IN ALL CAPS**.
Adhere strictly to all negative constraints: zero rhetorical questions, no "Picture this", no amplifying buzzwords, no comparative claims.

---

```markdown
# [PRODUCT NAME] — "[COMMERCIAL TITLE / CONCEPT]"
### Director's Treatment by Paul Regan
### Genre: [Genre of Commercial, e.g., Narrative Automotive, Cinematic Lifestyle, Tactile Comedy]

---

## OPENING HOOK
[Begin with a warm, conversational greeting directly addressing the reader—e.g., "Hello,", "Life, my friends...". 
Follow immediately with a bold, attention-grabbing statement that challenges conventional category thinking. 
Introduce your directorial vision with an enthusiastic, confident paragraph that highlights the project's unique heart. 
NO rhetorical questions. NO 'Picture this'. Use em dashes (—) and ellipses (...) for kinetic pacing.]

## BIG IDEA
[Succinctly introduce the core concept of the commercial. Ground it in relatable human experiences and genuine emotional truths. 
Explain how this idea elevates the agency brief's single-minded proposition into an unforgettable cinematic story. 
Use a pull quote or bold statement to anchor the central thesis.]

> **"[Insert Bold Declarative Thesis Statement]"**

## PREMISE
[Elaborate on the narrative approach. Detail the authentic moments, the humor found in the unexpected or mundane, and the organic role the product plays within the lives of our characters. The product must never feel artificially inserted; it must be the catalyst or natural companion to the human beat.]

## APPROACH
[Detail the filmmaking techniques that will create an intimate, cinematic feel. 
Contrast rigid commercial setups with tactile directorial methods—e.g., fluid handheld camera work that breathes with the actors, slow tracking dolly creeps, low-angle perspective, and immersive atmospheric depth.]

## TONE
[Describe the emotional tone of the spot: authenticity, charm, understated humor, warmth, and quiet affection. 
Explain the delicate balance between grounded realism and cinematic poetry. Emphasize that every emotional beat will feel earned rather than manufactured.]

## BLOCKING AND MOVEMENT
[Actor positioning and physical choreography as per the brief's requirements and transcript suggestions. 
Describe how characters navigate their physical environment, how proximity creates tension or intimacy, and how natural physical barriers (doorways, car interiors, countertops) frame their connection.]

## PERFORMANCE DIRECTION
[Directorial instructions for character emotions and subtext aligned with call insights. 
Explain how we will draw out honest micro-expressions, unscripted pauses, and subtle physical reactions. 
Focus on restraint—allowing silence and body language to tell the deeper story rather than exaggerated commercial acting.]

## CHARACTERS AND CASTING
[Outline the main characters, their distinct personalities, and the specific archetypes or actors who will embody them. 
Focus on faces with lived-in texture, genuine charisma, and relatable warmth rather than sterile model perfection.]

* **[Character 1 Name / Role]**: [Age, physical presence, demeanor, emotional posture, casting archetype.]
* **[Character 2 Name / Role]**: [Age, physical presence, demeanor, emotional posture, casting archetype.]

## THE LOOK
[Describe the comprehensive visual aesthetic, location architecture, textures, and styling.]

* **Body / Disclaimers Typeface**: `[Font Name]` — [Editorial placement, lower thirds, or legal compliance.]

### Wardrobe & Production Design
[Lived-in textures, natural fabrics (wool, linen, washed denim), authentic environments with genuine patina.]

## CINEMATOGRAPHY
[Camera packages, lenses, lighting ratios, and color grading discussed on the call.]

* **Lighting Style**: Naturalistic practical sources, soft window bounce, deep shadow roll-off, warm tungsten practicals
* **Color Grade (LUT)**: Film-emulation LUT preserving rich shadows, skin warmth, and brand palette accents

## EXECUTION
[The tactical shooting plan emphasizing speed, safety, and maximizing cinematic coverage. 
Detail how we will protect actor performance time, leverage optimal natural light windows (magic hour, dawn mist), and execute complex moves with a nimble, focused footprint.]
