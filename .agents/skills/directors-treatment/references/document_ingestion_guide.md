# Document Ingestion & Synthesis Guide: Call Transcripts & Agency Briefs

> *"A winning director's treatment does not merely summarize the brief. It reads between the lines of the agency call, identifies what the client is secretly terrified of, and proves that our camera will solve their problem with elegance, craft, and heart."*

---

## 1. Phase 1: Ingesting the "Call Transcript" PDF

The agency-director chemistry call is where the real commercial lives. Agency creatives often write conservative briefs to please brand managers, but on the call, they reveal their true artistic ambitions and personal frustrations.

### What to Extract from the Call Transcript:
1. **The Spoken Rhythm & Speaking Style**:
   - Note the colloquialisms, conversational shortcuts, and recurring idioms used by the director and agency creatives. Emulate this exact collaborative cadence in your treatment.
2. **The "Unwritten" Creative Itch**:
   - What did the agency creative director sigh about? Did they complain that the previous campaign felt *"too glossy and fake"*? Did they express a desire for *"real grit, honest laughter, or messy human moments"*?
3. **Client Landmines & Anxieties**:
   - What is the brand team worried about? (e.g., *"Make sure the product isn't lost in the shadows,"* or *"Avoid looking like a competitor's spot from last spring."*)
4. **Specific Production Ideas Floated**:
   - Locations mentioned offhand (*"What if we shot in Vancouver instead of a backlot?"*).
   - Music references or pacing ideas (*"Think early Springsteen or a raw cello pulse"*).
   - Casting philosophies (*"We need people with real weathered faces, not runway models"*).

---

## 2. Phase 2: Ingesting the "Agency Creative Brief" PDF

The brief is your legal and strategic bedrock. Every choice in your treatment must trace back to the brief's objectives.

### Key Strategic Extraction Points:
- **Product Name & Category**: Exact naming conventions, product tier, and key benefit.
- **Single-Minded Proposition (SMP)**: The one core truth the spot must communicate.
- **Target Audience Insight**: The emotional reality of the customer (not just demographic age brackets).
- **Mandatories & Legal Constraints**: Required product shots, packaging angles, disclaimer windows, or taglines.

### Brand Visual Asset Extraction (Mandatory):
You must thoroughly scour the brief or brand guidelines appendix for:
1. **Brand Color Palette**:
   - **Primary Brand Color**: Name + HEX code (e.g., `#0A192F` Midnight Navy)
   - **Secondary Color**: Name + HEX code (e.g., `#E63946` Crimson Accent)
   - **Neutral / Background Tones**: Name + HEX code (e.g., `#F4F1DE` Raw Bone)
   - *How to use in Treatment*: Integrate these exact colors into `THE LOOK` (wardrobe, production design, props) and `CINEMATOGRAPHY` (lighting gel tones, ambient color contrast, LUT notes).
2. **Brand Typography & Fonts**:
   - **Headline Typeface**: (e.g., *GT America Expanded Bold*, *Canela Roman*, *Futura PT Heavy*)
   - **Body Copy / Graphic Typeface**: (e.g., *Aperçu Regular*, *Univers 55*)
   - *How to use in Treatment*: Note how on-screen graphics, lower thirds, or final end-card typography will honor and elevate the brand's typeface identity.

## 3. Phase 3: The Cross-Reference Synthesis Matrix

Map extracted insights directly to the 13 required ALL CAPS treatment sections:

| Document Source | Extracted Insight | Injected Treatment Section |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brief SMP** | Relatable core benefit / emotional core | `BIG IDEA` & `PREMISE` |
| **Call Transcript** | Director's conversational greeting & bold industry insight | `OPENING HOOK` |
| **Call Transcript** | Discussion on handheld vs. studio rigs | `APPROACH` & `CINEMATOGRAPHY` |
| **Brief Target Audience** | Tone guidelines (authenticity, charm, humor) | `TONE` |
| **Call & Brief** | Physical staging and blocking discussed | `BLOCKING AND MOVEMENT` |
| **Call Subtext** | Nuanced emotional cues, unscripted moments | `PERFORMANCE DIRECTION` |
| **Call & Brief** | Character profiles, real people vs actors | `CHARACTERS AND CASTING` |
| **Brief Assets** | Extracted brand HEX colors & typography | `THE LOOK` |
| **Call Transcript** | Technical camera, lenses, lighting ratios | `CINEMATOGRAPHY` |
| **Call Discussion** | Shooting schedule, locations, efficiency | `EXECUTION` |
| **Brief Script + Call Ideas** | Shot-by-shot beats + Nano Banana 2.1 frames | `STORY TREATMENT` |
| **Director Voice** | Sincere commitment, personal passion | `CLOSING NOTE` |

---

## 4. Ingestion Checklist Before Writing

Before drafting the treatment, verify you have documented:
- [ ] Product Name and Commercial Genre
- [ ] At least 3 specific quotes or subtextual insights from the Call Transcript
- [ ] Primary, Secondary, and Accent Brand Colors with exact HEX / Pantone values
- [ ] Brand Headline and Body Typography names
- [ ] Mandatory product features or disclosures
- [ ] 3 to 5 key storyboard visual beats ready for Nano Banana 2.1 prompt generation
