# Content Density, Story Spine & Slide Structuring

This guide establishes the rules for converting long-form prose, director's treatments, and technical briefs into presentation decks. Presentations are an art-directed visual medium—never a verbatim copy-paste of document paragraphs.

---

## 1. The Two Density Modes

Before laying out a presentation, identify the primary setting:

| Density Mode | Setting | Design Rules | Limits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Speaker-Led / Cinematic** | Keynotes, live pitches, client calls | 1 core idea per slide. Large bold headline (26–36pt), dramatic visual dominance, generous negative space. Minimal body text. | Max 1–3 punchy bullet points. Max 25–40 words total. |
| **Reading-First / Executive Brief** | Decks sent async, director's treatments, review packets | Self-contained structured cards. Action assertion headline + 2 to 4 visual cards or comparison columns with supporting data and visuals. | Max 3–4 cards per slide. Max 50–60 words per card. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Overflow Invariant**: If content exceeds the density limit of a slide, **split it across continuation slides**. Never shrink text to illegible sizes or cram paragraphs into a single card.

---

## 2. Active Assertion Headlines

Every slide title must be an **active assertion** that states the takeaway, not a passive category label.

| ❌ Passive / Weak Title | ✅ Active Assertion Headline |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Kinematics` | `The Celebration Transforms into the Card in One Fluid Sleight of Hand` |
| `Lighting & Color` | `Warm Madison Square Garden Practical Light Meets High-Contrast Studio Glass` |
| `Choreography` | `Eight Synchronized Beats Connect the Clutch Shot to the Hero Lockup` |
| `directors_treatment` (Filename leak) | `The Commercial Arc: From Hardwood Glory to Cardholding Triumph` |

### The Kicker + Headline Pattern:
For executive presentations, use a 2-tier header structure:
- **Kicker / Category Tag** (10pt, bold, uppercase, accent color, tracking `+2`): e.g., `SCENE 01 // THE CLUTCH CELLY`
- **Primary Assertion Headline** (22–28pt, bold, primary title color): e.g., `Jalen Brunson's Signature 3-Point Celebration Anchors the Opening Beat`

## 3. Card Chunking Architecture (Anti-Prose Rule)

When converting narrative markdown sections containing multiple paragraphs into a slide:

1. **Identify the Core Claim**: Extract the overarching takeaway into the assertion headline.
2. **Decompose Supporting Evidence into Cards**:
   - Instead of 1 giant text block of 300 words, create **2 to 3 distinct visual cards**.
   - Each card contains:
     - **Card Badge / Subheader**: 1–3 words (e.g., `ANATOMICAL CHIRALITY`, `LENS PACKAGE`, `LIGHTING RATIO`).
     - **Card Key Assertion**: 1 bold sentence.
     - **Supporting Detail**: 2–3 brief bullet points or a concise 2-sentence description.
3. **Card Container Layouts**:
   - **2-Column Split**: Left text card (key insight) + right visual card (media / diagram).
   - **3-Card Horizontal Row**: 3 equal-width cards spanning the slide width with 0.3" gutters.
   - **Split 4-Grid**: 4 cards arranged in a 2×2 layout for multi-point comparisons or 4-beat progressions.

---

## 4. Preserving Director's Treatment Integrity

When adapting a commercial director's treatment:
- **Keep Sensory Language Punchy**: Convert dense paragraphs into vivid, kinetic bullet points.
- **Maintain Paul Regan Stylistic Invariants**: Kinetic em dashes (`—`), sensory immersion, zero rhetorical questions, and zero passive comparative language (*"not this, but that"*).
- **Synchronize Imagery with Text**: Every slide discussing a visual beat must have its companion 3D CAD plate or storyboard frame visible adjacent to the copy.
