# Production-car measurements

Measured on 2026-11-04 from eight production-car models (Sketchfab, author geyges; kept on the 3DTESTING drive,
not in the repo). Lengths in metres unless a ratio. Wheels were found from the tyre contact patches and a vertical
section through each tyre; glass from the materials. Every wheelbase is within 1.5 % of the published figure.
Use these to choose where in the SKILL.md ranges a car sits, by its closest relative here, never in place of the
published figures of the car asked for.
