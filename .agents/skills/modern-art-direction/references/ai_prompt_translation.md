## 2.5 The 4K Resolution & Zero-Text Invariants (Non-Negotiable Directives)

> **CRITICAL RULE**: Images are for **visual reality**; layout is for **words**. Images for layouts must **ALWAYS** be produced in **4K resolution** and must **NEVER** feature text.

### 1. Mandatory 4K Resolution Architecture
* **Standard 16:9**: Target **4K UHD** (`3840×2160` pixels).
* **Cinematic 21:9**: Target **4K Ultrawide** (`3840×1620` or `5120×2160` pixels).
* **Optical Fidelity**: Generates sufficient micro-contrast and edge sharpness for Retina displays, large client presentation monitors, and high-DPI projection without interpolation blur.

### 2. Why Baking Text into Images Destroys Art Direction
1. **Model Hallucinations**: Generative diffusion engines cannot reliably typeset; they produce misspelled glyphs, garbled letterforms, and nonsensical pseudo-English that immediately betrays the image as cheap AI output.
2. **Loss of Compositional Control**: When text is burned into pixels, it cannot be edited, re-ragged, kerned, scaled, or localized.
3. **Destruction of Visual Tension**: Great magazine design comes from the dialogue *between* a sharp vector font and a textured photograph. Flattening them into one mushy raster destroys that tension.

### 3. Mandatory Syntax to Append to Every Layout Prompt:
* **For 16:9**:
  `"4K UHD resolution (3840x2160), edge-to-edge optical sharpness, high dynamic range, fine physical surface micro-textures, 1/48s cinema shutter with subtle motivated micro-motion blur softening digital edges. Pure visual photography, absolutely zero text, no words, no letters, no alphabet, no fonts, no numbers, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, clean visual frame."`
* **For 21:9**:
  `"4K Ultrawide resolution (3840x1620), edge-to-edge optical sharpness, high dynamic range, fine physical surface micro-textures, 1/48s cinema shutter with subtle motivated micro-motion blur softening digital edges. Pure visual photography, absolutely zero text, no words, no letters, no alphabet, no fonts, no numbers, no subtitles, no watermark, no logo, clean visual frame."`

## 2.6 Coordination of Text and Negative Space in Layouts (The Planned Void)

An image generated for an editorial presentation or pitch deck is never an isolated artwork; it is an active half of a spatial dialogue with typography. When generating images for layout, the art director must orchestrate the **planned void**:

### 1. The Asymmetrical Thirds Rule (Anchor vs. Void)
* **Left-Aligned Slide Text (Standard 21:9 Editorial Spread)**:
  - The image prompt must explicitly command:  
    `"Subject composed strictly in the rightmost third of the frame, leaving the entire left two-thirds as an expansive, tranquil negative space void of dark board-formed concrete and subtle shadow falloff for editorial breathing room."`
  - The vector text column sits over or alongside this quiet void. Neither element competes for focal priority.
* **Right-Aligned Slide Text**:
  - The subject is anchored in the **leftmost third**, and the negative space opens on the right.

### 2. Eyeline and Vector Inward Convergence
* **The Gaze Trajectory**: A subject’s eyes, face angle, or walking direction must point **INWARD towards the typography**.
  - If the text is on the left, the subject on the right looks toward the left. This pulls the viewer's gaze from the image directly into the headline.
  - If the subject looks away towards the right edge, the viewer’s eye is led out of the canvas, abandoning the copy.
* **Architectural Vanishing Lines**: Architectural diagonals, cantilever edges, or raking light beams must slope toward the text column, forming physical visual rails that guide attention into the message.

### 3. Organic Scene Continuation (Negative Space Is Never Pure Black)
* **The Fatal Defect**: Generating artificial pure black (`#000000`) or flat graphic crop blocks for negative space. A flat black box is not photography; it reads as an artificial graphical border and ruins realism.
* **The Living Scene Rule**: Negative space in a photograph must **rarely be pure black**; it must be an **organic continuation of the photograph that is not busy**. Physical negative space consists of calm, low-frequency atmospheric reality:
  - Soft out-of-focus background architecture or distant trees.
  - Gentle twilight mist or coastal fog rolling across the ground.
  - Wet asphalt or concrete pavement trailing into soft shadow roll-off.
  - Smooth lime wash plaster or textured board-formed walls in ambient shadow.
* **The Five Tactile Optical Imperfections of Real Imagery**:
  Generative diffusion engines defaults to sterile, plastic AI sheen. To create images that feel authentic, organic, and familiar to the human viewer, deliberately command these 5 physical optical phenomena:
  1. **Subtle Motion Blur**: Minute camera motion or 1/48s cinema shutter drag that softens clinical digital edges and introduces physical temporal presence.
  2. **Tiniest Desaturated Tone**: Pull back synthetic digital saturation by 5–10% into authentic physical film stock palettes (e.g. Kodak Portra 400, Vision3 500T).
  3. **Subtle Chromatic Lens Distortion**: Natural optical aberration, gentle peripheral barrel softness, and delicate color fringing characteristic of real cinema glass (Cooke S4/i, Panavision C-Series, Leica Summilux).
  4. **Muddy / Lifted Blacks**: Real optical sensors and chemical film emulsions never crush to #000000. Shadows carry atmospheric bounce, optical flare toe, and tactile lifted charcoal/slate values.
  5. **Authentic Silver Halide Grain**: Fine physical film grain across the frame to establish organic material familiarity and break up digital pixel smoothness.

### 4. Micro-Motion Blur Settings (Scratchbox AI MCP)
* Always activate cinema shutter settings in Scratchbox AI MCP:
  ```json
  {
    "shutter_angle": 180,
    "motion_blur": 0.06,
    "quality": "4k"
  }
  ```
  This creates microscopic optical drag on moving edges (hair, fabric, camera pan), immediately breaking the synthetic hyper-sharp "AI render look" and imbuing the frame with genuine film mechanics.

### 5. Composing Negative Space for Full-Bleed Typographic Overlays
* **The Full-Bleed Spatial Canvas**: Photographs must be composed to span 100% of the canvas edge-to-edge. Never design images assuming they will be cropped or boxed into a split layout.
* **The 35/65 Asymmetric Flank**:
  - Anchor the hero subject (car, monolith, actor, product) within the right 35% of the frame.
  - The remaining 65% of the frame must be a calm, continuous, low-frequency atmospheric environment (e.g., misty wet concrete, shadowed lime plaster, deep shallow depth-of-field background) providing a natural, organic floor for typographic placement.
  - Leading lines and light falloff must slope inward toward the copy zone to create an architectural invitation.
* **Text Separation via Directional Optical Scrim**:
  - In presentation compilers (PPTX/HTML), overlay an invisible non-linear directional scrim (fading from 94% opacity on the copy margin to 0% transparency over the focal subject) so text achieves WCAG AAA legibility while the photographic scene remains fully continuous underneath.

## 2.7 The Section Exemplification Engine: Translating Assertions into Visual Proof

When generating an image for a specific section of a presentation, pitch deck, or treatment, the image must **never be generic ambient wallpaper or stock filler**. It must serve as **tangible visual proof** that exemplifies and substantiates the core argument made in that exact section.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    THE SECTION EXEMPLIFICATION PIPELINE                     │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. EXTRACT THE STRATEGIC CLAIM  │ Identify the core thesis, tension, or      │
│                                 │ mechanical mechanism in the section text. │
├─────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. RUN THE GENERIC DETECTOR     │ "No. That's the obvious version."         │
│                                 │ Reject the first 3 predictable stock tropes│
├─────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┤

│                                 │ vectors inward, enforce 4K & zero-text.   │

```
