# Orthogonal Curation Matrix: Fresh Takes on Layout, Typography, Color & Craft

> *"The interesting exercise is to collect references separately for layout, type, color, and interaction. You can then build an original visual language from those four decisions rather than borrowing an entire website's personality."*

---

## 1. The Core Doctrine: Orthogonal Decoupling

When designers or AI agents copy a single website (e.g., cloning a sleek SaaS landing page or a trendy agency portfolio), they inherit that website's clichés, compromises, and derivative fatigue. 

**Orthogonal Decoupling** decomposes visual taste into four independent, decoupled mathematical and aesthetic axes:

$$\text{Visual Identity} = \left[ \text{Axis 1: Spatial Layout} \right] \times \left[ \text{Axis 2: Typographic Tension} \right] \times \left[ \text{Axis 3: Material Chromatics} \right] \times \left[ \text{Axis 4: Micro-Interaction & Craft} \right]$$

By sampling each axis from a distinct cultural, historical, or physical reference library, you synthesize a non-derivative aesthetic universe.

### Axis 2: Typographic Tension & Scalar Dynamics
*Primary Repositories: Fonts in Use, Typewolf, Future Fonts, Pangram Pangram, Fontshare, Velvetyne, Typescale, Fontpair.*

| Cult Favorite | Core Genius | Abstracted Design Invariant |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fonts in Use** | Historical context, real-world cross-disciplinary pairing archives. | **The 3-Layer Polar Friction Model**: Pair typefaces of starkly different historical origins (e.g., 18th-century Didone display + mid-century Swiss grotesque body + contemporary monospace datum). |
| **Future Fonts / Velvetyne** | Experimental, strange, expressive typefaces in development. | **Sculptural Display Scale**: Treat idiosyncratic display fonts as visual art objects ($44\text{pt}-80\text{pt}$), never as standard body text. |
| **Pangram Pangram / Fontshare** | Commercial-grade workhorse grotesks and display families. | **The Invisible Reading Layer**: High-x-height, neutral grotesks (*Plus Jakarta Sans*, *General Sans*, *Satoshi*) optimized for effortless reading at $11\text{pt}-14\text{pt}$. |
| **Typescale** | Mathematical hierarchy, modular scales, responsive ratios. | **Strict Modular Scale Steps**: Ban arbitrary font sizes. Use defined mathematical progressions (Major Third $1.250$ or Perfect Fourth $1.333$): $10 \rightarrow 13 \rightarrow 17 \rightarrow 22 \rightarrow 28 \rightarrow 36 \rightarrow 48 \rightarrow 64\text{px}$. |

#### The Polar Tracking Formula
Typography must dynamically balance optical mass. As scale expands, tracking contracts; as scale shrinks, tracking expands:

$$\text{Tracking}(fontSize) = \begin{cases} -0.03\text{em} \text{ to } -0.05\text{em} & \text{if } fontSize \ge 40\text{pt} \text{ (Display Headlines)} \\ 0 & \text{if } 14\text{pt} \le fontSize < 40\text{pt} \text{ (Reading Body)} \\ +0.15\text{em} \text{ to } +0.25\text{em} & \text{if } fontSize \le 10\text{pt} \text{ (Uppercase Metadata/Folios)} \end{cases}$$

### Axis 3: Material Chromatics & Role-Based Luminance
*Primary Repositories: Happy Hues, Realtime Colors, Picular, Khroma, Coolors, Adobe Color.*

| Cult Favorite | Core Genius | Abstracted Design Invariant |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Happy Hues / Realtime Colors** | Palettes applied directly to live UI elements rather than isolated chips. | **Role-Based Chromatic Staging (The 60-30-8-2 Rule)**: Allocate color strictly by spatial surface ratio and functional hierarchy. |
| **Picular** | Google-style search engine extracting color from words and subjects. | **Physical Materiality Derivation**: Extract palettes from tangible matter (unbleached linen, patinated copper, cast zinc, raw travertine) rather than mathematical wheel offsets. |
| **Adobe Color / Khroma** | Harmony rules, APCA contrast validation, luminance accessibility. | **The Neutral Subtitle Invariant**: Subtitle text must NEVER be computed via chromatic wheel shifts (e.g. $+30^\circ$ split-complementary offsets that turn warm gold into electric neon lime-green). Subtitles must permanently remain neutral Titanium/Slate tints (`#94A3B8`). |

#### The 60-30-8-2 Chromatic Staging Rule
* **$60\%$ Canvas Base**: Deep Obsidian (`#07090E`), Blackened Steel (`#0E1117`), or Warm Bone Paper (`#F6F4EE`).
* **$30\%$ Monumental Type**: Pure Specular White (`#FFFFFF`) or Deep Sumi Ink (`#181C24`).
* **$8\%$ Structural Subtitles & Borders**: Neutral Titanium/Slate Tint (`#94A3B8` Slate-400 / `#64748B` Slate-500).
* **$2\%$ Singular Motivated Accent**: Exactly ONE high-chroma hue (Swedish Gold `#E5A93C`, International Klein Blue `#002FA7`, or Signal Red `#FF3B30`), restricted strictly to micro-kickers, active indicators, or datum lines.

```
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 4. Agentic Execution Checklist

Before producing any design artifact, presentation slide, or web interface:
1. **Never copy an entire reference website.**
2. **Decompose the design problem into the 4 orthogonal axes.**
3. **Audit against the Invariants**:
   - [ ] Is photography docked flush to boundaries (no floating postcards)?
   - [ ] Are strategic statements unboxed on canvas (no card fatigue)?
   - [ ] Is display typography tightly tracked (`-0.03em`) and metadata widely tracked (`+0.2em`)?
   - [ ] Are font sizes anchored to a disciplined mathematical typescale?
   - [ ] Is color staging following the 60-30-8-2 rule (neutral subtitles, singular $2\%$ accent)?
   - [ ] Have all decorative toothpick underline bars been purged?
   - [ ] Are running folios isolated strictly to text columns with zero photo collision?
   - [ ] Are bullet points structured into numbered datum shelves (`01 //`)?
