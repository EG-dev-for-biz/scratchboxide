📷 How Google Vector Map Data Enriches COLMAP & GLOMAP

In photogrammetry and Structure-from-Motion (SfM), accurate 3D vector map data from Google provides geometric priors that solve scale drift and camera degeneracy:

  • Road Centerline Splines & Curb Ribbons: Provide metric scale constraints and vanishing line directions (29.0° and 119.0°).
  • Cadastral Parcel Prisms & Building Facades: Constrain plane-sweep stereo and provide structural bounding volumes for dense depth estimation.
  • 15 COLMAP Survey Stations: Each camera station in our rig has calibrated ENU coordinates (X, Y, Z) and optical forward vectors aligned to Google's vector grid.
